About Chess Club

I started Chess Club in S1 because I wanted to give people a place to play chess. Unfortunately, not many people attended, and my teacher eventually allowed people to do other activities instead. I stopped going, but I decided to restart Chess Club properly in S2.

When and Where?

Chess Club takes place in Room 2-043 on Thursdays and Fridays. Chess boards will be available, but you can also bring your own set.

You can play against a friend, another member, or even play online. Beginners are welcome, and I can help teach you how to play.

Why Chess?

Chess helps you think ahead, plan your moves, make strategic decisions, concentrate, be patient, and solve problems. It can also be mentally tiring, which makes it a great challenge.

A Little About Me

I started playing chess when I was five. I play chess every day for at least 30 minutes and aim to get at least five checkmates every day.
