About Chess Club

Welcome to the Chess Club! We meet regularly to play, learn, and improve our chess skills together — from beginners to advanced players. This app includes a built-in tournament-style chess clock so you can time your games with presets or fully custom time controls, including increments.

Use the Chess Club button on the home screen to jump straight into the clock and start a timed game with a friend.
